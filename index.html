Porsche Unleashed Score Reset: January 17, 2003
Happy New Year to EA Racing fans across the globe! As usual and on the new 6 month cycle, the scores for Porsche Unleashed will be reset this Friday, January 17, 2003. This maintenance should not take more than an hour and will mean the servers will be taken down while this happens. Fortunately the maintenance is brief and the servers will be put back up shortly after the score reset is accomplished. See you in the pits!

EA Online Racing Servers back in business!
Both the High Stakes and Porsche Unleashed Servers have been successfully moved to their new home and racing can begin again in earnest. We apologize for the delay in getting your home for online racing back online but expect many more good racing experiences to be had in both games effective immediately. To discuss the performance of the new servers as well as get racing tips and advice from other online racers, we encouraged you to join High Stakes players at Need for Better Speed and Porsche Unleashed players at Racerplanet.

Porsche Unleashed host enhancements.
The “Gulliver” cheat code and others that have made for unfair advantages have been disallowed from play in the game servers. Use of this cheat code can only be experienced in offline play now. The “Smash Up” code will remain for those Porsche drivers who prefer to abuse their rides. Also, Porsche Unleashed hosts have been updated too! The new servers maintain fewer connections per user and are designed to handle greater player loads. This is the first version of the new servers, so expect behind the scenes bug fixes and optimizations. As mentioned earlier, scores for Porsche Unleashed will be started from scratch after the move is completed.
