Find your Motor City Online answers quickly in our Knowledge Base. Search by category, keywords or phrases, or submit your own questions. Game play questions not answered in the Knowledge Base should be directed to MCOhelp@support.ea.com or simply use the convenient form located at the Knowledge Base.

  

    		  
  Please visit Tech Support for help with installation, patching, lag, connectivity, client errors, sound problems, display problems, and game performance issues with Motor City Online. If you have an issue that is not covered in the technical support knowledge base or the Known Issues section of this site, please send email to: technicalsupport@support.ea.com. Also, you can call us toll free at (866) 543-5435, 24 hours a day. For international customers, please dial (512) 434-6740.

  

    		  
  Please visit the Account Support for assistance with billing issues, payment options, Account Access Transfers, creation of new accounts, editing existing accounts, and password help.

Account Assistance: (866) 543-5435 (option 2, then 1) for assistance with login, password, updating accounts, and other account related issues.

Billing Assistance: (866) 543-5435 (option 2, then 2) to purchase an additional account, alternate payment options, registration problems, cancellation requests, or to reactivate. If you are dialing from outside of the United States, and need to speak to a billing representative, please dial: (512) 434-4357.

For questions about banned, suspended or hacked accounts, please mail the following. Note: there is no phone support available for these issues.

Hacked@ea.com - If you suspect your account has been hacked.
MCOadmin@ea.com - For questions with a suspended account notification.
Banned@ea.com - For accounts that have been terminated for TOS violations.

 

    		  
  For help with the MCO message boards (lost passwords, unable to post, etc.), please email Account support at eamembers@custhelp.com , and be sure to include your EA.com account name (do not send your password) and your board username, if you have one. You can also find many questions and answers that apply to the MCO boards at Tech Support.
