In addition to the information you can find within the game about how to play or how to form a club you will find many links to external web sites that can address your game play questions, account support questions, technical support, and Game Master (GM) support. In some cases, you will be afforded the option to fill out a form that will direct our attention to your urgent in-game issue. Please do not misuse these forms as invalid requests for GM assistance that could be found in the Knowledge Base is against the EA.com Terms of Service. Please review the knowledge base for questions to typically asked questions.

  

    		  
  GMs serve many functions within Motor City Online: mediator, problem solver, police, and customer service. The most important thing to remember about a GM is that they are not here to change the rules of the road. While we will extract people from situations in which they are not able to continue playing, GMs should not be used as divine intervention. We do not fix cars, give cars or MCO cash, or otherwise tell you how to play the game. In fact, we believe the less interference from the GMs, the more rewarding a game Motor City Online will be. We want to encourage players to solve problems on their own, within the community. We firmly believe that the more players come together and form a community, the less they have to worry about anti-social behavior. However, certain times a GM must step in and clear up misunderstandings or remove harmful players. We ask that at those times you respect that a GM is only doing what is best for the game.

Please remember that Game Masters can only assist you with issues inside the game world. GM's cannot assist with technical support or account issues. For these issues, please visit the Tech Support and Account Support links under Game Help on the menu to the left.

  

    		  
  It is important to understand that there are times when a GM might ask you to stop doing something, or may ask that you do something differently. Please remember that if a GM asks you to do something, they are asking for the benefit of the game and the community. We must ask that you comply with any request a GM may have of you. Even if a policy is not listed, you should still do as a GM asks. Should you disagree with the GM, or feel they are not being fair, you should pursue this through e-mail by sending it to MCOconcerns@ea.com. We will review the actions of the GM and the circumstances of their request. Please do not argue with the GM or make repeated calls into the queue to discuss the matter further. This only hampers the GMs ability to help other players within the game.

  

    		  
  If you are within the game and are offended by the behavior of another player, please ask the player nicely to tone it down. If this request is not heeded, please fill out the “Request Help” form located under the “MCO Help” tab in the upper right-hand corner of the game window. Fill out the form as fully as you can by properly identifying the nature of the problem you have had with another player as well as identifying the player in one of the 4 boxes provided at the bottom of the form. Please note that you can potentially complain about up to 4 other players in the game that could all be part of making the game play experience miserable.

Depending on how severe the situation is, you may expect a GM to arrive on the scene to warn or remove problem players. You will not likely be able to see them in this act however as GMs have tools that allow them to do this subtly. In most cases, problem players will be dealt with by email and/or temporarily suspended from the game. Repeat offenders will have their EA.com accounts terminated immediately and will be banned from Motor City Online forever. Please check out the Motor City Knowledge Base, the Rules of the Road, and the EA.com Terms of Service for details on behaviors that are not tolerated with the Motor City Online game environment.

  

    		  
  I understand there are things GMs will not do. Is there a list of commonly asked questions that GMs simply can not help with?

  
 
GMs will not alter a player's stats or skills.
GMs will not alter a player's car or any item in their garage.
GMs will not change a player's name.
GMs will not alter times on a track for a player.

GMs do not reimburse players for lost or stolen items/MCO cash.
GMs do not interfere with club politics nor do they impede any particular game play style such as (but not limited to): mechanic, club leader, racer, etc.
GMs can not force auctions or the parts catalog to spawn inventory, or force any other resources to respawn MCO items such as cars, parts, etc..
GMs do not provide game internals such as how many races, records, cash, or parts another player has.
GMs are forbidden from discussing account actions that have happened to any other player.
 
  Can a GM see my inventory in the game?

No, GMs cannot see your garage or inventory items inside the game. If you have been verbally harassed, it is vital that you page a GM using the Harassment paging option. Please see the Harassment Articles in the Knowledge Base for more complete information about our Harassment policy and how to report verbal Harassment.

 

    		  
  At some point in time it's possible that in MCO, just as in real life, someone will try to scam you out of your hard-earned possessions. Here are a few tips that might just help prevent that scammer from being successful.

Proceed Cautiously.

Trading Items
Secure Trade Windows: Take time to check the items in the window to make sure you're getting exactly what you're expecting to get. Always check to make sure that EVERYTHING you are trading for is in the trade window. You can check the item(s) details by clicking the "More Detail" button. There is NO REASON why you have to click the "Trade Arrow" for them to see the item(s) in the trade window regardless of what they say.

Cross-District (Server) Trades: We cannot become involved in any way with cross-district trades, either to assist in the trade or to investigate possible scams after the fact. Motor City Online supports securely trading items within a district only, and any trades that players seek to accomplish across districts are done at their own risk. See previous developer Team Comment for more details.

General Advice & Comments
In Case of a Scam: Use the in-game HELP button, located in the upper right hand corner, immediately. Do not change chat channels prior to filing a help request. Make sure that you provide details in the summary and description sections and list all players involved.

Be Cautious About "Proof": Don't accept ICQ logs, screenshots, and similar out-of-game evidence as proof of someone's honesty. These things are too easily falsified or inaccurate. Plus, many times these things may be trojan viruses designed to steal your account login information.

Club Scams: Many times unscrupulous players will deceive club members to 'loaning' them their car, parts or money. They will promise to return the item, pay you back or use other methods to sway you into believing they will not scam you. Be extra careful when loaning your items to other players you do not completely trust. It may serve your best interest to take a collateral item when loaning out your inventory. This way, if the other player does scam you, you are not completely without a method to race or play.

Be Extra Cautious with Valuable Trades: If you are making a "big ticket" deal, and about to sink your life savings into a car or performance item(s), it may be a good idea to "take some notes" before the trade. Look up the character using the character profile search option.

Note: Many scammers create new characters (with extremely common names) for their scams, and delete those characters 7 days after creation in an attempt to make it more difficult to track the perpetrator of the scam.

Buying Items Outside the Game: If you buy something Motor City Online-related outside the MCO game system, such as on eBay or some other auction site, and get scammed, this is a legal issue and not an in-game issue. Players shouldn't page or e-mail EA or MCO staff with information or complaints about this type of fraud, for this is out of their domain, and we are not responsible for deals made outside the game.

Gentlemen's Agreements: EA/MCO can not uphold any form of agreement that was made by players in MCO. The most common gentlemen's agreement is a promise to return cars lost due to a pink slip race. If you enter a pink slip race and lose, be prepared to lose your car. We can not return lost cars.

Ultimately, if the other trader is not willing to do simple things to prove the legitimacy of the deal, such as build the engine using their parts, allow you to pay them when you receive the part(s)/car: drop the deal. There are other auctions, parts, cars, and this person may be hoping to scam you.

GMs will not return items lost through scams, so we encourage all players to take an active role in protecting themselves against scams. Use the in-game tools provided for secure trades, and investigate before you buy!
