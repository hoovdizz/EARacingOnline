MCO maintains that the player experience should be one in which the player may enjoy all aspects of game play without impediment due to either offensive or otherwise harassing behavior. MCO also maintains that players who do not follow these broad community standards are subject to removal from our service. Below are our general Rules of the Road as defined by behaviors that are not considered within our community standards. EA.com and all of our moderators appreciate your cooperation and assistance in making Motor City an enjoyable and fun place to play online. Please show consideration for all Motor City citizens by adhering to the following rules:

  
 
The use of any profanity, sexually explicit, offensive, unlawful, harmful, threatening, abusive, harassing, defamatory, vulgar, obscene, hateful, racially, ethnically or otherwise objectionable language toward any other player or in game may result in the immediate termination of your EA.com account.
The creation of any EA Account Names or Persona Names with any profanity, sexually explicit language, sexual innuendo, or racial, ethnic, or religious slurs may result on the immediate termination of your EA.com account.
Please be considerate at all times of members playing games in Motor City Online. Disruptive behavior, such as making lewd comments, or continuously entering a race and then leaving before you complete it, may lead to account penalties.
Cheating, abuse of game mechanics to gain an unfair advantage, usage of "hacked" or inappropriately modified cars, exploiting any bug in the Motor City Online product or the Motor City Online Service, and/or communicating the existence of any such bug (either directly or through public posting) to any other user of the Motor City Online Service is prohibited and can lead to the immediate termination of your EA.com account.

Please avoid excessive shouting (use of all caps) or flooding/spamming (continuous posting of repetitive text).

Impersonating a Motor City Online Representative, an employee of Electronic Arts, and/or false representation of yourself as an Electronic Arts employee or EA Racing Moderator will result in immediate termination of your EA.com Account.

Threatening other players, MCO Moderators, and Electronic Arts employees is prohibited and will result in immediate termination of your EA.com account.

Please report violators of the Rules of the Road or Terms of Service using the “MCO Help” functions to capture chat to report any abusive users.

Marketing, promoting, advertising, or make any other form of solicitation (including pyramid schemes and chain letters) on the Motor City Online Service or through the Motor City Online Web Site is prohibited.
Member's right to use the Service is personal to Member. Member may only be an individual. Member may not be a corporation or other business entity. Member agrees not to resell or make any commercial use of the Service, without the express consent of Electronic Arts.
Modifying any part of the Motor City Online Service or Web Site that Electronic Arts does not specifically authorize you to modify if prohibited.
Arranging for the exchange or transfer of any pirated software or other contraband while you are on the Motor City Online Service or Web Site, or use the Motor City Online Service or Web Site for any other inappropriate purpose is subject to immediate EA.com account termination.
Organizing Clubs that are based on, or espouse, any racist, sexist, anti-religious, anti-ethnic, anti-gay, or other hate-mongering philosophy is prohibited and will result in immediate EA.com account termination.
Posting or communicating any player's real world personal information within the Motor City Online Service or through the Motor City Online Web Site or bulletin board is prohibited and will result in immediate account termination.
Giving false information or hide any information, such as email address or billing information, which is required at the time you register with the Motor City Online Service, is prohibited. It is the player’s responsibility to keep this information current.
Interfering with, hacking into, or deciphering of any transmissions to or from the servers running the Motor City Online Service will result in immediate termination of your EA.com account.
Any member of Motor City Online that has had their account permanently closed due to disciplinary actions is prohibited from using the Motor City Online Service without written consent from EA.com or Motor City Account Admin.
The use of third party programs to gain an advantage in Motor City Online is strictly prohibited and will result in account penalties.
 
  Motor City Online reserves the right to change its policies at any time. Please help keep Motor City Online a fun place to play! Please visit the EA.com Terms of Service for additional detail. Specific details on behaviors actioned in Motor City Online can also be found in the “Rules and Policies” section of the MCO Knowledge Base.
