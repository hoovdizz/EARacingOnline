MCO Player Petition
In response to the recent MCO player petition, Rick Geiger, VP of Customer Relationship Management, would like to share the following message with all players of Motor City:

We understand the concern you have demonstrated in regards to the resource allocation for Motor City Online and we thank you for your note. Electronic Arts, as a company, often has to make difficult decisions in the allocation of funds for product support. We do our best to get the most effective mix, but we understand that everyone is not always satisfied with the decisions we make. Electronic Arts is continuing to support MCO and we have no current plans to alter that. We hope you will continue to enjoy the game. Please feel free to give me or Ted Avery at 512-434-6817 a call if you would like to discuss this.
