NEW! - Can I pay with PayPal?
Yes. Motor City Online customers can now take advantage of their PayPal accounts to pay for their accounts! If you do not have a PayPal account, but were looking for a new option to pay for your Motor City Online account, you may set up the account for free at www.PayPal.com. The payment amount is $13.99 for 30 days of game time and $38.99 for 90 days. Please see our Knowledge Base for more details.

How do I get another Motor City Online account so my family / friends can play with me?
If you are an existing Motor City Online player, you may obtain an additional account for $14.99 each. This will include the first month of play. Additional months are subject to our regular monthly subscription fee. Please see our Knowledge Base for details.

Can I pay for MCO subscription with checks?
Motor City Online now gives you the opportunity to pay for your Motor City Online account using our new Alternate Payment Options (APO), Checks by phone. To take advantage of this payment option, please call our Billing Department at 1-866-543-5435 (hit option 2, then option 2) during our hours of operation: Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM CST (GMT -06:00) and Saturday and Sunday, from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM CST (GMT -06:00). When calling, please have your checkbook and driver's license number available. This offer is currently only available to residents inside the U.S.

How much is the MCO subscription price?
Motor City Online has a monthly subscription fee of $9.99. The first month of this fee is included in the purchase of the game. After the first month you can use your credit card to buy more time. Rates and content are subject to change.

May I use the same credit card for my subscription and my Motor City Online account?
Yes. When you create your new EA.com member name for Motor City Online, you may use the same credit card you are using for any other member name to pay for the service.

May I use a Pepsistuff Subscription Reward Code for my subscription to Motor City Online?
No, you may not redeem your Pepsistuff code for Motor City Online. You are free to redeem your Pepsistuff code using a new EA.com member name and enjoy the great offer for the EA Need For Speed Web Racing. Available only through Pepsistuff.

What happens at the end of my 30 days free trial?
When your 30 days free expires, your credit card will be billed for the monthly subscription price for Motor City Online. Manage your account by visiting EA.com. You may cancel your account by contacting our Billing Department at 1-866-543-5435 (hit option 2, then option 2). If you are dialing from outside of the United States, and need to speak to a billing representative, please dial: 512-434-4357.

Where can I find information on my Motor City Online account and my EA.com subscription account?
Please visit My Info section after you have logged into EA.com for details and information about your current subscription status. Have more questions? If you have additional questions regarding your EA.com member account, please contact Account Support at 1-866-543-5435. If you are dialing from outside of the United States, and need to speak to a billing representative, please dial: 512-434-4357.

Why do I need to create separate accounts to use these services?
At this time, EA.com is unable to provide for multiple subscriptions under one member name, so we ask that you create a new member name/account for each service you wish to acquire. We appreciate your understanding and are working to give members the ability to manage all your accounts under one member name and login.
