What's the objective of Motor City Online?
Motor City Online was meant to be a massively multiplayer online persistent state community for car lovers, loosely modeled after the great car "Mecca", Detroit. As such, there isn't really a singular objective. You can work towards becoming a Top Ace driver. You can work to become the wealthiest person in Motor City. You can run your own club of fellow car enthusiasts. You can become an expert Motor City auto mechanic by learning all about the parts system. On top of all this, you can make friends and talk to live people from all over the continent.

What different gameplay types are there in Motor City Online?
Motor City Online offers a multitude of racing experiences, designed to appeal to everyone from the "Sunday Driver" to the most rabid of hot-rodders.
Sponsored Racing: Race on specific tracks with cars that are provided to you by a "sponsor". This is a good way to learn a track without worrying about wrecking the car you bought with your own hard-earned Motor City dollars. These races are only run for MCO cash and points, and include both a Time Trial mode and regular multiplayer racing modes.
Circuit Racing: Race on Motor City's professional, sanctioned racetracks for MCO cash and points.
Street Racing: Race in the streets of Motor City. This is where an unlucky wager could lose you your car!
Open Trials: Race against the clock on any of Motor City's Street or Circuit racetracks, for points and MCO cash.
Drag Racing: Pit your car against other racers in a test of nerve and reflexes.

Is Motor City Online a "Racing Sim" or an "Arcade" type of game?
Motor City Online is actually a combination of both "sim" (simulation) and "arcade" styles. However, it leans more heavily toward the idea of "sim" due to the game's persistent economy, the ability to modify your car, and the implementation of more realistic car physics and damage. The most arcade-like experience in Motor City is to be had in a Sponsored Race of any kind - this is because in a Sponsored Race players are provided with a car, so they don't have to pay for repairs if it gets damaged in the course of the race.

Does Motor City Online have classes and skill points like other online role-playing games?
Not exactly. Skill points and classes in most RPGs allow your persona to improve over time. In these games, until you reach a certain number of points your persona will not improve. In a racing game, the players are directly in control of their own vehicles. A wheel twitch at the wrong moment can make even a Top Ace driver lose a race! Therefore, it's not practical for our game to limit players in this fashion.

Racing in Motor City Online does grant you experience points, which will allow you to advance in level and rank. The higher rank you are, the more MCO cash you receive in your weekly salary, to do with as you wish in the game.

Is there any sort of advancement cap or limitation in Motor City Online?
No. Players will be able to advance in levels indefinitely. There is no higher rank than Top Ace, however.

How many personas can I have on one shard?
You may have one persona on a shard at a time.

How long do I have to wait after creating a persona before I can delete it?
Once you've created a persona, you must wait seven days before you can delete it.

Will cars randomly break down in Motor City?
No. All car breakdowns at this time are completely dependent on part wear and damage. Take care of your car, and replace worn-out parts, and you'll have a long and fruitful racing career.

Can I customize my car in Motor City so that it looks unique to others?
Your car can be customized with paint jobs and accessories purchased from the Detail Shop, or with certain add-on parts that will change the outer appearance of your car. Another nice touch (also the easiest and cheapest) is to personalize the text on your license plate!
