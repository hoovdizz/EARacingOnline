5/8/02, 5:41 PM PDT (GMT -07:00)
Update 6 is Live! Please check Update Notices 6 for final list of features, changes, and bug fixes.


4/23/02, 11:13 AM PDT (GMT -07:00)
Update 6 is now In Testing. New Cars and Parts are not yet available.


4/19/02, 5:27 PM PDT (GMT -07:00)
As the development of Update 6 is wrapping up, the team has released a more up-to-date list of what the players can expect. Please note this list is always subject to change, based on test results.

What's New:
Team Trials

Classified Ads

Player Index

Local News

Hub View

New Track - Goose Pointe

More Cars - 4 new cars.

More Parts - 50-80 parts, including tires, springs, spoilers, shocks, a new engine block family, and numerous engine parts.

Club Title - Club Captains and Officers can create and give each member a Club Title.

Club Re-registration - Registered Clubs have the ability to change logo / acronym with a fee.

Persona Profile - Each player has a limited text space to put in a custom profile. The profile is displayed while viewing persona.

New Vehicle Power Classes - Power Classes SA, SB, SC, and SD. These new classes begin at a power to weight ratio of 0.20.

End Race Camera - The end race camera now shifts to the next player who has not yet crossed the finish line.

Populated Races - Available from the Racing drop-down menu. Populated Races will display a list of racing lobbies that are most active at the moment.


Changes / Improvements:
Improved Progression - Race rewards tuned to ease level progression. Tracks and Righteous Rides are unlocked by level instead of rank.

Class Restricted Turf Wars - Turf Wars are now Class Restricted. Each track requires a different class of car.

Expanded Turf Wars Rewards - Top 5 Turf War clubs will now receive rewards upon weekly tally. The Captain, Top 5 Racers, and all Members will receive in-game cash reward.

Trade Interface - A warning dialog will appear if content of a car has been changed while viewing details.

Club Races - Club races now carry PvP race rewards.

Expanded Time Trials Rewards - Top 10 Time Trials Champions will now receive rewards upon weekly tally, instead of top 3.

Expanded Top Dog Rewards - Top 5 Top Dogs will now receive rewards upon weekly tally, instead of the number one player.

Car List Sorting - Cars are now sorted by year in all pull down menus.

Garage - The garage now exists in a pop-up window, combined with the active car selection pop-up. You can also view car details from this menu.

Player Hosted Auctions - The auction has been limited to 3 days maximum with new prices.

Parts as Prizes - Winner of the race will have a random chance of winning a part, instead of a guaranteed chance.

Email - Each in-game email can now handle up to 2,000 characters, including spaces.


Bug Fixes:
Fixed the shifting problem when the car hits air.

Fixed a bug where parts would be completely worn out after a race.

Fixed the Auction paging bug where the same page was visible after clicking page 1, then page 2, etc.

Fixed a problem with ghost car states, which will improve the effects of "Lag".
