7/23/02, 7:04 PM PDT (GMT -07:00)

I meant to write a team comment on this awhile back but could never find a good time for it. Now our third round of dev chat is around the corner, let me take the opportunity to say a few things.

What is a dev chat?
If you are new to our dev chat, commonly known as House of Commons on Stratics, it is an event where players can ask developers (folks like me) questions concerning Motor City. The Q & A happens in a moderated format, where players message their questions to a Stratics staff, and the staff will organize and pose the questions to us for answer. This is an important part of the chat because without the moderators, the developers will have to sort through 100+ questions while trying to keep a conversation going.

If you missed any of our previous chats, you can find the chat logs on MCO Stratics under House of Commons.

How do I participate?
MCO dev chat is open to anyone and everyone. To join our chat, you will need to use an IRC (Internet Relay Chat) client. A popular choice is mIRC, which is available at www.mirc.com. In addition to downloading and installing the IRC client, you must also connect to the Stratics chat server at irc.stratics.com. We lost many participants from previous chats because they were connected to the wrong server. For a complete how-do guide, please visit Stratics Chat FAQ. Very soon, MCO Stratics will provide a Java version of the client, which means only a web browser is required to participate.

Can I ask anything?
Sometimes we will host a general chat, in which the developers will try to answer all MCO related questions. Often on we will pick a discussion topic ahead of time. This gives us a more focused and meaningful discussion. Players can also better prepare their questions in that scenario. If your questions do not fit the discussion topic, the moderators will filter those out for the team.

How often do the chats occur?
Ideally, we would like to see this event happen regularly. But we have not settle on a schedule yet. Until then, please look for news announcements on MCO website or in-game World News (under Community Center). We will post any chat event at least one week in advance.

What happens to the unanswered questions?
Since we don't type in supersonic speeds, it is unlikely that the team can answer every single question from the players. In our last chat, for example, we received about 150 questions. In order to answer them all within an hour, we only have 24 seconds for each question! The good news is that the nice folks over at Stratics will email us all the questions at the end of the event. And if we see some popular issues, we will try to address them through team comments.

I hope that covers all of the frequently asked questions in regards to our dev chat. I would like to take this moment to thank Stratics for hosting the events for us, and please remind me NOT to let Ghoughakka order the pizza. He likes strange toppings.

See you in our next chat!
