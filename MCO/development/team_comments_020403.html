PORSCHE UNLEASHED
 


    		  
4/3/02, 6:28 PM PST (GMT -08:00)

First, I want to introduce myself to those that do not recognize my name. Yikes here, at your service. I'm still attached at the hip of Motor City and have been for the past 3 years. Many of you that were around in the time of beta testing may remember the Yikes vs. Jack Ryan legacy, and the many message posts I've made in attempt to solve problems. I will be conducting the testing effort on Test Center and will be active in bug hunting and along with all those that participate.

Second, I want to update you all on the status of the opening of Test Center and what we plan to accomplish there. Initially, we will be looking at features and bug fixes for Update 6. For those that do not know what is planned for Update 6, click here to read about it. I'm very excited about the new features and bug fixes, and we're on track for release soon. Soon is the best I can promise because we still have a few variable dependencies to work through in order to finalize a date. Rest assured however, that when we start, the new items slated for release will be fun to test indeed.

Third, I want to explain how the setup will operate. There will be separate download that will auto install your current copy of Motor City to another area on your local hard drive. This will mean that everyone who wants to participate will need another 800 MB of free space. It will not disrupt or affect your current installation, but, you will only be able to run one at a time. Once we release the installer for download, we will open Test Center on a schedule that I am hoping will not conflict with peak play times on the real servers.

Bugs will be reported in game, feedback and discussions can be posted on the message boards, and you will be able to test new update features before we release them. Just another way to ensure that Motor City growth and expansion will go forward with minimal issues and maximized potential.

See you there!!

~~ "Yikes"
