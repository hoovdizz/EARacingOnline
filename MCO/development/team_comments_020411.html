4/11/02, 2:14 PM PST (GMT -08:00)

After the overwhelming responses from interviewing Ghoughakka, BigBen from City Center picks up his pen & paper again and interviews Cadillac! Read on for details!

BigBen: Cadillac, you were the first person from EA that I ever saw post on a discussion board (It was MCOHeadquarters many moons ago), how long have you been involved with the NFS/MCO line?

Cadillac: I picked up support of EAOR (EA Online Racing) after a few of the High Stakes players were successful in getting heard about their concerns with online racing. At the time there was support for the offline parts of these games but there was no support resources assigned to the online part of High Stakes and Porsche Unleashed. Online support is all I had ever done so my team (Summer of '01) was the obvious choice to do this since we were supporting the EA Games channel on the fledging EA.com site. Of the two games, I played PU the most but got to love the HS community much more.


BigBen: What is your role in the MCO world?

Cadillac: I am the support manager for the team assigned to Motor City Online in the Customer Relationship Management Department, specifically, the Player Relations Department. In a nutshell, I am the point man for MCO with the studio, billing, marketing, tech support, and the Game Masters.


BigBen: We all know you race under different names. Has there ever been a time that you wished you could step into a phone booth and reveal your true identity?

Cadillac: Not really. As soon as folks know who I am in the game, there goes my fun and it turns back into the job. Does that make sense? I mean when I go to play, I want to play. Answering all the millions of questions about white letter tires and trucks starts to remind me of being on the clock. Plus, I would rather experience the game from a player perspective to know where things need to be improved.


BigBen: Have you ever raced me under a different persona name?

Cadillac: Nope. You don't race on the same shard that I do. :)


BigBen: It's common knowledge that there were a set of cars finished at the end of Beta developed for release over time. Is there a grand timeline over which they will be released?

Cadillac: Yes, but I really do not have any input into that. Ghoughakka and Trouble are on that. I can say that the new cars do string out over time but that's all I know.


BigBen: Has there been a pattern to the release of cars?

Cadillac: Umm... I actually do not know. How embarrassing! Well, maybe not since I am the support manager after all.


BigBen: How much of the MCO staff's time is spent maintaining the game, and how much is spent developing new content?

Cadillac: Since the recent changes in staffing at the studio we have had to "do more with less" as the saying goes. Maintenance of an online game has to be the most important thing in my opinion, but so is new content to a game like MCO. It is for that reason that a lot of the new content we are planning is already "in the can". All that remains is figuring out how to release it as bug free as possible. I would say the ratio is pretty even.


BigBen: Is that ratio likely to change as the game goes on?

Cadillac: Well, that is likely to depend on how bug-free an update is. Making the current version as stable as it can be is priority number one before we look at adding new code.


BigBen: We would all love to see lag reduced. What can the players do to help?

Cadillac: What can players do to help? All I can really think of that may help is to assist us in identifying routers in their route from the game hosts to their machine that are within the Above.net network. I suggest using a tool that has widespread use with the Ultima Online players called UOtrace. The details on how to use it along with IP's to their favorite game shard is located here:

http://motorcity.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/motorcity.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_sid=FqjjZebg&p_lva=&p_faqid=2905

Players can trace their routes and submit their results to us via the help pages located here:

http://motorcity.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/motorcity.cfg/php/enduser/ask.php


BigBen: What steps are EA taking to reduce lag?

Cadillac: We are constantly looking at what we could do from our end to reduce the amount of lag but this is a very hard, if not impossible, problem to kill. As new technology on network packet transfer becomes known to us, I am sure the development team will make use of it. Time will tell but am I not the expert here.


BigBen: How does the MCO staff feel about the "Launch Busters"?

Cadillac: My opinion of them is that this is a great example of the community taking the initiative to solve a problem on their own. Community policing, or at least activism likes this, is a great way to garner attention without being too obnoxious about it in my opinion.


BigBen: What is happening in the MCO community that has surprised you?

Cadillac: The emergence of the amount of players who focus on collecting. Sure, we all love a good race but sometimes the application of brakes or gas in a specific turn eludes all but the best drivers especially when there are more than one in a row (like in HH). A recent thread started by a player on the boards that asked about what percent racer, collector, or mechanic are you was started. It showed me that there were a lot more collectors and mechanic than racers out there. I was pleasantly surprised by these results.


BigBen: Time to come clean on the AI drivers. They're sneaking in Class C cars into the Class D races, aren't they?

Cadillac: Hehe I have to laugh. All I can say is that those inanimate suckers always take the same lines I do. That should be enough to tell you about how good I drive.


BigBen: Any hopes of seeing them banned? Some of them have truly nasty notions of fair play...

Cadillac: File a complaint and we will investigate their activity. : )


BigBen: Are you getting tired of answering these questions yet?

Cadillac: Well, given there was no deadline and I was able to spread out my answers over a couple days, no.


BigBen: You shared the answers to some interesting statistical questions about MCO, such as the 65 Mustang being the make of car that has collected the most wins. What other surprises did the number crunchers reveal?

Cadillac: There was actually a good bit of data that came to light about the game and the players but we are saving that information to use in trivia contests. If I told you now that would ruin the fun!


BigBen: You already know how many questions the MCO players have for you. Do you have any questions for us?

Cadillac: None at the moment.


BigBen: Thanks for the time Cadillac!

~~ BigBen & Cadillac
