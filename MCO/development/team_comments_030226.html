HIGH STAKES
PORSCHE UNLEASHED
 


    		  
2/26/03, 9:00 AM PST (GMT -08:00)

Dear Citizens of Motor City,

Today we made an important announcement about the future of Motor City Online. Before I jump straight into answering the thousands of "whys" out there, please let me re-iterate what this announcement means to you:

Server Downtime
The four MCO servers (Uptown, Midtown, City Center, and Crossroads) will continue to operate and will be maintained until 8/28/03. Starting 8/29/03, 12:01 AM GMT, these servers will be disconnected and no players will be able to log into them. I'd like to remind you that GMT stands for Greenwich Mean Time. Depending on where you are located, 12:01 AM GMT on 8/29/03 might be 8/28/03 to you.

Account Billing
After 7/16/03, no accounts will be billed for the remainder of the time that MCO is available. If you have an active account, you may continue to play MCO for free until the servers are disconnected. If your credit card is billed after 7/16/03 for Motor City Online related services, I strongly urge you to contact our Account Support department.

Veteran Rewards
The Veteran Rewards program will continue monthly until 8/20/03, which is the third Wednesday of the month. Players who reached account age of 1 year old by 8/6/03 (first Wednesday of August) will receive their Veteran Rewards on 8/20/03. However, Veteran Rewards will not be handed out on Test Center on the second Wednesday of every month. See below.

Test Center
During the week of 3/2/03, Test Center will be disconnected and no longer available to players. The purpose of Test Center is for players to verify new features and content before their official releases - this is now no longer needed.

Community Programs
Also during the week of 3/2/03, a majority of the community programs will be discontinued. Specifically, these programs are Fan Site Submissions, Player Paint Job Submissions, Player Story Submissions, First Gear Club, Player Event Sponsorships (Prize & MCO Cash), Track of the Day, and Weekly Staff Event. However, Player Event Submissions will remain active until the last day of MCO. Event organizers can submit their events per the submission guidelines to have the events posted on the MCO website.

Message Boards
Message Boards have always been an integral experience for the Motor City community. As a result, the Message Boards will continue to operate until 8/29/03.

Ultima Online / The Sims Online
If you are currently an active Motor City subscriber, you will receive an email offer detailing how to obtain a free copy of Ultima Online (UO) or The Sims Online (TSO) if you are over the age of 13. Similar to MCO, these games take place in online persistent state worlds with thriving communities. Though you would not see a loaded hot rod speeding through either of these worlds, you will have the opportunity to fight dragons in UO or run the best disco club in TSO. Being an active UO player myself, I enjoy it as much as MCO. If I had a clone, I'm sure he would be playing TSO when he wasn't doing my laundry. Both games come with 30-day free trial period. I recommend you to accept this offer and give your new game a test drive. But keep in mind this offer expires on 7/16/03, and your MCO account will be closed when you redeem your copy of the free game.

Why?
Now that all the key topics have been addressed, I'm going to attempt to explain everything that has led up to today's announcement. And to those Trouble (and anti-Trouble) fans, I can't help myself but to say things in the typical Trouble-ish way. If that's not your cup of tea, I apologize in advance.

Motor City began its development cycle as a regular, non-online product - an extension of our popular Need for Speed brand. However, back in 2000, we decided that Motor City should lead EA into the world of online racing with its rich content and robust parts system. To reflect this bold change in direction, Motor City was renamed Motor City Online.

I joined the development team in our Seattle studio shortly after the change. MCO was on the drawing board. The entire production team brainstormed through thousands of ideas on how to make the game work online. Meanwhile the engineering team began to build the massive server infrastructure to support player communication and transactions. Not to mention our art team who worked endlessly to add more content and to re-design the interface for all these changes. In all my professional life, I have never before worked with such a talented group.

In October of 2001, against many odds, the team delivered MCO just in time for the holiday season. The initial sales data was encouraging. However, we were soon faced with a new challenge only applicable to online games - how to keep the community thriving.

MCO was (and still is) very different from a lot of subscription-based games because of its competitive nature. It was evident that the game was quickly dominated by skilled players. Anyone who can modify cars and race well succeeded while the less-skilled players failed. New players were faced with more defeats than helping hands. Though the overall subscription base was growing, we also experienced a substantial outflow of existing subscribers.

The team then set out to improve the game. One of the perks of being an online game was our ability to update it as we saw fit. Features such as Team Trials, Club Registration, Public Race Records, etc. were quickly put into design and then development.

Unfortunately, the product was losing steam. By the time these features were released, the sales of MCO already began to slow down. In the world of computer games, retailers promote and sell the latest and greatest. MCO no longer fit that category. As our shelf space shrunk, so did the inflow of new players. The MCO community stopped growing.

At the end of the day, we had to make the business decision that resulted in today's announcement. Our production resources are valuable, and the EA team that has been supporting MCO needs to go to work on other games.

On behalf of the MCO team here at EA, we would like to thank you for making MCO an unforgettable experience. You have brought us much enjoyment and you will leave us with many fond memories. And knowing our game was enjoyed around the clock and around the globe, no other developers could wish for more. When we reach the end of this road, and when the servers are disconnected, a part of us will go with it too.

Thank you again. Sincerely,

~~ "Trouble"
