PORSCHE UNLEASHED
 


    		  
12/17/01, 11:11 AM PST (GMT -08:00)

Finally! This is another feature that has been at the top of a lot of people's wish lists, although now that we've publicized it some players seem to have concerns. Here's our reasoning: With the added complexity of Car Classes, we knew that a lot of would-be mechanics would be out there building cars to meet specific Power to Weight numbers. We felt that the addition of this feature was essential to encourage more people to participate in this area of the game.

The other benefit to this feature is that it will allow players who want to take the time to explore all the parts in the game the opportunity to do so. Concerns have been that this might take the 'challenge' out of the game. Well, in a way that's true. Now you'll know for sure just how much power that 1,000 cfm carburetor will give you over your 850 cfm carburetor without having to buy it! But you still won't know what other combinations of parts might give you a better result. So the mystery still remains, to a certain extent. In most cases you're still going to want to consult with your best buddy, the veteran MCO car mechanic!

~~ "Ghoughakka"
