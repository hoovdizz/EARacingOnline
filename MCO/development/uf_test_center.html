Test Center is now Live.


3/5/02, 9:17 PM PST (GMT -08:00)
Have you ever wondered what new features and new content were in the works for Motor City Online? Would you like the opportunity to test out the new game modes, new cars, and new parts before they hit the streets? Well now's your chance! That's right, the developers for MCO are working on opening a Test Server for the public. Anyone with an active MCO account would be allowed to log on to Test Center and give all the latest content a spin. Players who test out the newest build would be able to send feedback to the MCO development team. In this manner, balancing issues will get resolved before-hand, and bugs can get fixed before they corrupt the live shards.

How it Works:
Every player with an active MCO account will be allowed to log onto the Test Center.

Test Center will be used for strictly testing purposing. Because of this, there will be no continuity to personas created on the Test Center. There will be persona wipes.

At any time, Test Center may be locked for internal testing purposes.

At any time, all persona levels and cash may be wiped for testing purposes.

To help test specific areas of the game that may not be accessible to everyone, we may award levels, parts, cars and cash at various times to all personas on Test Center. Keep in mind though that the cash and levels you gain may be wiped at any time.

Feedback will be collected via an in-game Bug Report form. The developers will ask for feedback relating specifically to the new content or new bugs that exist on Test Center.

An additional 800MB of hard drive space is required to access Test Center.
