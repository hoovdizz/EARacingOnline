GAME HELP
 

    		  
  The First Gear Club is a group for new gear heads just entering the game, designed and organized by seasoned veterans of Motor City. Membership in the First Gear Club will provide a more fun and competitive Motor City experience for everyone involved!

What's in it for new citizens?
  1. Get a head start with key trade secrets from Motor City experts
  2. Obtain parts to improve and optimize your cars
  3. Benefit from pro tips and advice
  4. Be invited by a veteran to join a more established Car Club

What's in it for the veterans?
  1. Set the stage for bigger, better community
  2. Recruit the most talented rookies for positions in your permanent clubs
  3. Tear up the road with more races than ever before
 

    		  
  New Players (Level 1-10): Go to the Community Center of your server (City Center, Uptown, etc.) and do a search for First Gear Club. Contact Club Captain and/or Officers to get an invite into the club!

Veteran Players (Level 11+): Contact the First Gear Club Captain and/or Officers and offer your assistances to these new players!
