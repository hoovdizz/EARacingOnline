12/20/01
6:31 PM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  Prices at Righteous Rides
Shortly after the patch, it was brought to our attention that some of the prices were set too low in the new Righteous Rides dealership. The prices were low enough to allow some very dedicated players to mule cash by buying and selling these cars, then deleting personas and starting over. To prevent this possible exploit, we have readjusted the costs of the lower level cars in Righteous Rides to a level that should prevent exploiting. We apologize for any inconvenience caused by this change.

(GMT -08:00) 		  MCO Clubs - Fun for Everyone!
Motor City Clubs are thriving! Currently there are 85 clubs in existence with memberships of 30 or more, across all districts. We're happy to say that many clubs seem to be emphasizing teamwork and courtesy towards others over individual competition, which may be why they're gaining and retaining members. Many of these clubs are groups of people who just enjoy hanging out, racing and talking about their cars together. Check the Club Index on your district and look up members of clubs you're interested in - who knows, after meeting some of these guys and gals you might end up in a club yourself!

12/5/01
2:04 PM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  Being a Top Ace!
If you're hanging around the tracks and streets of Motor City, you may see certain players who are ranked "Top Ace". These players are high-level racers who really know their way around the tracks. If a Top Ace challenges you to a race, expect to get schooled. However, "schooling" isn't generally bad for you - most Top Aces have a relatively low "Insurance Risk", meaning that they don't get into too many crashes, and prefer a "clean race". Some Aces and Top Aces even enjoy playing the role of mentor-- helping newer racers learn the tricks and special maneuvers that can cut valuable milliseconds from their overall times.

Watch for these folks (and other helpful players) and soak up their knowledge, so that you too can become a Top Ace!
