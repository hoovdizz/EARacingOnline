11/14/01
10:51 AM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  Racing to the Top in Motor City!
Thousands of players are logging into Motor City Online every day. But what are they doing? According to our sources, they're racing, making MCO cash, and striving to be the most powerful racer in the town. Reasonable goals, I'd say... and people are well on the way to reaching them! There are currently 33 players across all districts who are Veteran Racers (level 20+), and we have our first three Aces - TC Mores on City Center Atlantic, Spud on Crossroads Atlantic, and Wrayth on Uptown Pacific! If you see these guys, give them a big cheer (and if you're a newbie, don't challenge these guys to a race, they'll probably wipe the streets with you!). We'd also like to congratulate the following two racers who have completed over 2000 races within the first two weeks: Gabriel and Cobra! Now THAT'S a LOT of driving. Come on guys, go race something besides the dirt oval! (Just kidding!)

Keep up the great racing everyone, we'll have another update on the State of the City later this week. Log in and make your mark!

 
11/8/01
4:24 PM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  Motor City Online Clubs are Booming!
To date, there are 43 clubs with a membership of more than 20 across all Districts of Motor City. City Center Atlantic has the most total clubs at this time, with 80 listings in the Club Index (found under the Community dropdown at the top of your game screen). The club with the most members at this time is "MOPAR Madness", with 61 total members on Midtown Pacific. The second largest club is the Uptown Pacific chapter of "Road Warriors", with 60 members... very close! When asked why clubs are so popular, members tell us consistently that it's a great way to get started as a new player, it's more fun to hang out with a smaller, close-knit group of people with similar interests, and it opens up some new game areas to you (Club Racing and Turf Wars). You can learn more about the benefits of joining a club by visiting the in-game Help area (MCO Help >> General >> Community)!

 
11/7/01
11:14 AM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  Turf Wars Raging!
Racers on the City Center Atlantic district will notice a certain name plastered across almost every multiplayer race lobby in Motor City: TEAMGOD. How did that name get onto every track? Turf Wars, that's how! TEAMGOD is a 32 member club on City Center Atlantic that decided to claim every last piece of ground they could get their grubby little paws on as their own in the first week of play. Now, it's up to the rest of the clubs to go in, challenge them, and knock them off their pedestal. Well... they can try, anyway. Check out the rules for Turf Wars in the game by clicking on MCOHelp >> General >> Community >> Club Races. Good luck to all the contenders in the upcoming weeks!

 
11/6/01
3:43 PM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  Motor City Online Keeps Growing!
Motor City Online has been open to the public for only six days and we've already noticed a thriving community beginning to take shape. For example, across all districts (server sets), there have been over 180 clubs created. Some of these are what we would consider "Chapter" clubs, which have a presence on all districts - a great idea! The largest club in Motor City Online currently is the Midtown Pacific chapter of the club called "MOPAR Madness", with 59 members at the time of this article!
