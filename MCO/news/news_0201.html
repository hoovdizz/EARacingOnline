1/25/02
6:18 PM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  Important News for Clubs
An update has been performed this evening addressing two important problems that affect clubs: Turf War Awarding and Club Disbanding. After this update, Turf War winners whose Club Name appears on the track they competed for will receive the appropriate cash for the win. Soon, past winners who were not correctly processed will be getting back-pay for their efforts.

In addition, after this update, clubs will no longer be "disbanded". A club will only be removed from the Club Index after the final member has quit. In the case when the Captain quits or deletes his/her persona, a new Captain will be automatically selected from the list of remaining club members (officers first by seniority, then regular members by seniority). We hope this resolves some problems clubs have been experiencing, and thank you for your patience while we tracked down these issues.

(GMT -08:00) 		  Important Notice from the Support Team
Your Motor City Online Support team is dedicated to resolving issues and helping players who file a complaint in-game. Unfortunately, we are unable to do this effectively if players do not have a valid email address tied to the EA.com account that they use to access Motor City Online. This has also been a problem for players who should be receiving warnings and/or notifications of an MCO suspension. If you have filed a complaint using the in-game Request Help pages and have not received a response, please check to make sure your email address is valid, current AND accepting mail. You can correct your account information on the EA.com website using the following link: https://www.ea.com/myinfo/eacom/reenterpwd.jsp. Use your MCO username and password to access this site. The GM Team appreciates your cooperation!

(GMT -08:00) 		  MCO Wins in 2001!
Happy New Year from Motor City! Thanks to great support from our players and public, MCO has won the Gamespy 2001 PC Sports Gamers' Choice Award! Here's a quote from Gamespy's article: "EA has worked endlessly on Motor City Online with a concept that no one thought they could pull off, and three years later, those early screenshots that we first drooled over have become a reality; a game that for innovation alone is worth a look from even the most casual of gamers." We truly appreciate the support of all our fans and we're positive that our updates in 2002 will bring continuing enjoyment to everyone.

MCO was also nominated in the GamePen PC Awards for BEST PC RACING GAME OF 2001 and BEST ONLINE GAME OF 2001, receiving a Racing "Cream of the Crop" badge.

1/4/02
7:01 PM PST
(GMT -08:00) 		  MCO Goodies for January & February
In a couple weeks, we'll be publishing another round of major upgrades to the auto parts selection. We'll release several big block engines and all the associated components, including some awesome high performance stuff. We'll fill you in on the exact date and details when testing nears completion. But one thing is for sure: if you've been chomping at the bit for more power (for either small blocks or big blocks), some awesome upgrades are on the way. In further good news, this patch will cost you zero download time, since all changes can be made on EA servers rather than to the data on your hard drive.

Sometime in mid-February, if all goes well with development and testing, we'll release what we hope to be our best download yet. Here are some things we HOPE (no promises) to patch into the game: Parts-As-Prizes in multiplayer racing (various auto parts available for winning at different tracks), a new Cooperative Racing Mode (i.e., you and your buds try to score "team bonus" times), "ghost car" improvements in multiplayer racing (so you don't collide with "laggers"), more cheat prevention measures, improvements to Club Creation and Club Index, ability to create Club Decals and put them on your car, a bunch of user-interface improvements, three classic pickup trucks and a new track called Hazard Road (a backroad, high-speed, seat-of-the-pants street track). Again, each of these goodies is a work-in-progress and will only be released after successful testing, because game stability comes first. We'll provide more details on each feature as we close in on completion.
