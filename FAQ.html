Welcome to EA Racing's Frequently Asked Questions guide. We hope the information here is useful in the event you have questions or experience difficulty racing online.

Q: Where is my CD Key Located?
A: The CD Key is no longer needed to create an account or register for online racing. This step has been removed from the account registration process. If this has prevented you from registering in the past, try again now!

Q: Where do I find the download cars?
A: Visit our download cars page by clicking here .

Q: I am having difficulty downloading cars. Sometimes the download will stop and give me the message "Connection with server timed out" in the middle. The site does not resume broken downloads either so I have to start all over again.
A: Due to the overwhelming popularity of the download cars, the server occasionally cannot keep up with the traffic. The only solution is to try again.

Q: I can't use download cars with the new NFS:HS patch. Why is this?
A: The latest patch was intended to improve online racing by preventing the use of modified 'cheat' cars among other things. It has had the unfortunate side effect of preventing the use of 'unofficial' download cars in the single player game as well. If you wish to use these type of cars with your game, you will need to reinstall the game without the latest patch. Please note that this will prevent you from participating in online racing, however.

Q: I get a "Racing Servers are not responding. (-1)" when I try to go online with earacing. What gives?
A: This means that your computer is unable to reach the EA Racing servers. This could be because the servers are down, you aren't connected to the Internet, you have a bad install, or you don't have a route to the servers.

If this error appears after the persona selection screen, then the servers are probably down. Please send email to the site administrator and let us know.

If it appears when you enter your user name and password, then you probably can't locate the machines. To verify this, bring up a DOS prompt and enter the command 'ping login.earacing.com'. If you get a reply, then the servers are up and something else is the problem. Again, send mail to the site administrator and let us know your exact configuration so we can try to help you. If you don't get a response, contact your ISP and ask them to try and help you figure out why you can't see the machines.

Q: All my points are gone! The server reset them to zero. What happened?
A: This error is caused by a communications problem between your computer and the EA Racing servers. If you get this error, back out to the login screen and try again. DO NOT race if this appears, or your point loss will be permanent. If you back out and log in again, your points should reappear.

Q: I never received email confirmations for my racing user names. It's been ## days/weeks/months since I registered my account! When should I expect them?
A: They should have come right away. Please send mail to the site administrator, giving the user name you entered, and we'll send you your password again.

Q: When I sign up for a new earacing account I receive an Internal Server Error.
A: This is caused by an abort in the program that creates the account. Your account may or may not have been created. If this happens, please send mail to the site administrator giving the name you were attempting to create and we'll let you know if the account was created.

Q: I've just downloaded the new 4.44 patch and when I attempt to log on from the game I receive an "Unknown Error". This happens when the game is "Checking for Updates" and I am unable to connect.
A: If you receive this error, please send us an email describing the problem and containing a copy of your Push_Log.txt and PBA_Log.txt files so that we may investigate the problem. These files can be found in your NFS 4 directory, wherever you may have installed it.

Q: When I try to connect I keep getting error messages that the user name/password are invalid.
A: User names and passwords are case sensitive. This means that the name John is different than the names JOHN, john, and JohN. Check your spelling and case and try again. Is the caps lock light on? If you're still having trouble, please send mail to the site administrator giving the name you were attempting to use and we'll let you know if it's correct.

Q: What is the Online Racing Beta Program?
A: The Online Racing Beta Program allows our customers to play Need for Speed High Stakes online while we develop and fine-tune our Internet racing technology for future products.

Q: How do I join EARacing?
A: Simply create an account by visiting our Account Setup pages.

Q: How much does EA Racing membership cost?
A: EA Racing membership is free. You just need to own a copy of Need for Speed High Stakes or Need for Speed Porsche Unleashed and have Internet access in order to play online.

Q: Sometimes when I play, the cars seem to "warp" or "pop" on and off of the track. What causes this?
A: When you race online, you may see cars "warp" or pop in and out of play along the track. This is a known issue inherent to our positional updating system. We're working very diligently to remedy this problem and present a smooth racing experience.

Q: What is latency?
A: Latency is essentially the time it takes for information to travel from one source to another and the effect that amount of time has on a user's experience.

When you experience high latency, you'll see a notable delay or difference between what you input as to what you see happen onscreen; sometimes cars will warp, you might see other cars jump from one place to another, etc.

When you experience low latency, you'll note that your input and what happens onscreen are somewhat simultaneous: less car warping takes place, etc.

For those playing Need for Speed High Stakes over the Internet, there is a latency indicator next to each player's name. When your indicator is green, it means your latency is low and that you'll see good results playing. Yellow means you're experiencing a moderate amount of latency while a red indicator shows that your latency is high and you might see some adverse effects racing.

Latency can be caused by heavy Internet traffic, your connection speed, your ISP, amount of traffic being handled by the racing server, etc.

We're doing our best to reduce the impact of latency issues on the gaming experience of EA Racing.
